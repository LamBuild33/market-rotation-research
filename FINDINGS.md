## 2026-10-16 — Notebook 03: Top-5 test (Part A, build years 1999-01-04 to 2020-12-31)

Tests the dashboard's ranking idea: each month, hold the five sector funds with the highest past-month change. Compared with the equal-weight sector average, after 0.10% trading costs.

### Part A predictions (written before running anything)
- **T1.** After 0.10% trading costs, the top-5 portfolio beats the equal-weight sector average by at least 1.0% per year (average excess per holding period x 12).
- **T2.** The top-5 portfolio beats the sector average in at least 13 of 22 build years.
- **T3.** The top-5 portfolio beats the sector average in more than 50% of holding periods.

Each is confirmed if the stated threshold is met and contradicted if not.

With only 9 funds (before late 2015) the top 5 and bottom 5 share one fund.
- **Plain-language summary:** each month, buy the five sector funds that rose most over the past month and hold them until the next month's trade. Compare with simply holding all the sectors equally.

### Part A results (run 2026-10-15, notebook `02_volume_check.ipynb`)
- **Data range and setup:** build years only, 1999-01-04 to 2020-12-31; 23 symbols (BTC-USD left out), adjusted close and volume. 263 checkpoints, one every 21 trading days from 1999-02-03; the last one with a full next-month outcome ends 2020-12-14. 4,939 labeled symbol-checkpoints (the same 4,939 in both views). Rules copied from the dashboard exactly. Volume came from the same Yahoo Finance download as notebook 00 (no new download; see 00 data notes). Neutral share of observations is 31%; "Doesn't confirm" is only 10% (478), so its numbers are noisy.
- **Plain-language summary of the test:** every month-end checkpoint, each fund is tagged by whether its past-month move came with volume on the same side. We then ask whether the next month moves the same way. A coin flip is about 50%; the all-checkpoints baseline is 51.4% (see why below).
- **V1, Confirms continues at least 5 points more often than Doesn't confirm: CONTRADICTED.** Confirms 50.98% (2,915 observations) vs Doesn't confirm 52.09% (478): gap **-1.11 points**, the wrong direction. Neutral: 52.07%; baseline 51.43%. A rough 95% range for the gap, resampling checkpoint dates (extra context, not a prediction): -7.7 to +4.9, so even the best case in that range does not reach +5.
- **V2, Confirms beats Doesn't confirm in at least 15 of 22 years: CONTRADICTED.** Only **11 of 22** years (2003, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2020). Year gaps swing widely, from -32.8 (2002) to +17.9 (2003) points; mean yearly gap -2.2, median +0.05 (each year has just 15 to 30 "Doesn't confirm" observations).
- **V3, Doesn't confirm continues less than 50% of the time: CONTRADICTED.** It continued **52.09%** of the time (249 of 478).
- **Why the baseline is above 50%:** continuation mostly reflects the market's upward drift, not volume. After an up month the next month is also up 59.8% of the time (2,962 cases); after a down month it falls again only 39.2% of the time (1,965 cases). Average next-month change is similar across labels: after up months +0.44% (Confirms), +0.38% (Doesn't confirm), +0.69% (Neutral); after down months +1.22%, +0.34%, +1.05% (positive means the fund went up the next month, even after down months). So the label does not separate good from bad next months. 12 observations had a past-month change of exactly zero (counted as Neutral, never "continued").
- **By group (price view; gap = Confirms minus Doesn't confirm):** 11 sectors -1.9 (n 1,397 vs 267), 4 industries -4.4 (444 vs 93), other markets +3.2 (919 vs 105), SPY +4.2 (155 vs 13, too few to read). No group reaches +5, and the groups disagree in sign, which looks like noise.
- **Vs average sector view (second view):** Confirms 49.46% vs Doesn't confirm 47.48%, a gap of **+1.98** points; baseline 49.2%. Confirms beats Doesn't confirm in 14 of 22 years. Groups: sectors +4.1, industries -2.9, other markets -0.5, SPY +1.1. This view leans slightly toward the dashboard's idea but is still far below the 5-point bar and under 15 of 22 years, and is not the main test.
- **Cutoff check (information only; no best cutoff picked), price view, Confirms minus Doesn't confirm:** 50/50 -2.50, 52/48 -1.58, 55/45 -1.11, 58/42 -4.25, 60/40 +0.80, 65/35 -3.89 points. Confirms stays between 49.95% and 51.12% at every cutoff; Doesn't confirm bounces between 50.3% and 55.1% (as few as 52 observations at 65/35). Vs-sector view gaps: -0.49, -0.50, +1.98, +1.45, +1.42, -2.36. No cutoff comes close to a 5-point gap in either view, so the result does not depend on the 55/45 choice.
- **Caveats:** checkpoints use the same funds at once and funds move together, so 4,939 observations are far fewer independent ones (the resampling range above is wide for that reason). Sector funds are also part of the average they are compared with in the vs-sector view. SMH before 2011-12-20 is the old Semiconductor HOLDRS, and XLRE (from 2015) and XLC (from 2018) have short histories (see 00 data notes).
